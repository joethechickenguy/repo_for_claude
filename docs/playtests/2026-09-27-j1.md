## What changed

- **Milestones inside long builds** (a slowdown and a log line halfway through): Stage 1 crucibles
  (`first_bead`), pot bellows (`first_bellows`), arsenical copper; Stage 2 far seam, trip hammer,
  finery forge, Newcomen engine, boring mill; Stage 4 steam turbine (`first_rotor`), welding
  (`first_arc`); Stage 5 launch complex now in quarters (0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0).
- **Stage 2 labor**: far seam 600k → 450k person-days, blast furnace 500k → 350k, sand casting
  300k → 200k (the engines wait on it). Trade-off lines on coppicing, lime burning and the finery
  forge, so they count as decisions.
- **Hauling**: rails and canals now scale the hauling bar (`pressure_scale:`, new node field:
  x0.2 and x0.5, the -80% and -50% their cards promise) instead of a fixed offset. The offset
  held in Stage 3 and failed at Stage 5-6 tonnage (`pressure_red:haul_labor` x16 and x19 in the
  first j1 run).
